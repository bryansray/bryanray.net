I'm Bryan Ray. I'm a software developer and technical leader who enjoys turning ideas, business needs, and complicated processes into software that works well for the people who use it.

Over the years, I've helped build companies, led development teams, designed software systems, and worked alongside people as a manager, architect, mentor, and developer. Those roles have taught me that good software is about more than the code. It comes from understanding the problem, balancing business goals with technical constraints, and helping a team carry an idea all the way through to a finished product.

I care about thoughtful design, practical solutions, and leaving things better than I found them. My work has covered everything from application architecture and development processes to infrastructure and product design. I've spent most of my career working with C#, TypeScript, JavaScript, Elixir, Ruby, SQL, and the ecosystems around them, but the tools have always been secondary to solving the right problem.

Outside of work, I'm a father. I also write, read, make the occasional video, and follow whatever curiosity has my attention at the moment. This site is where I share some of what I'm building, learning, and thinking about along the way.

I enjoy working with thoughtful people who have reasonable expectations and interesting problems to solve. If you need help with a development team, codebase, architecture, infrastructure, or software design, I'd be glad to hear from you.
