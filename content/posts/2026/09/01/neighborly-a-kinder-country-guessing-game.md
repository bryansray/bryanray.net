Over the weekend, I built [Neighborly](https://neighborly.bryanray.net/) for my daughter Kylie. It is a geography game where you try to find a mystery country, one guess at a time.

Each guess warms up the map and tells you how far away the mystery country is and which direction to travel. If you get stuck, Neighborly can point you toward countries that border your closest guess. There is a new daily puzzle, plus practice mode when you want to keep playing.

I built it with SolidJS and SolidStart. More importantly, it was a fun excuse to make something playful for Kylie—and maybe help us both learn a little more about the world along the way.
