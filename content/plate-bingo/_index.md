# Plate Bingo

**Spot every state plate.**

Plate Bingo is a small iPhone app for tracking the US state license plates you've spotted on the road. Mark each state as you see it, watch your bingo card fill in over the course of a road trip, and — if you want — share progress with the rest of the family in real time.

## What it does

- **All 50 states** in one clean list, sorted however you like.
- **One tap to mark a plate seen.** Swipe right on a row, or tap into the detail screen and tap the button.
- **A map pin for each spotting** so you can remember where you were when you spotted Wyoming for the first time.
- **Share with family.** Send a one-tap invite link to your kids or your spouse. They'll see your spotted plates in their own copy of the app.
- **Syncs between your iPhone and iPad** through your own iCloud account.

## What it doesn't do

- No advertising.
- No analytics or tracking.
- No third-party data sharing.
- No selling your data, ever.

The app collects only what it needs to work, stores it on Apple's iCloud and a small Supabase server I operate, and lets you delete everything from inside the app with one tap.
